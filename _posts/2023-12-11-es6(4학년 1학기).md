###### The former Gyeonggi Provincial Government Complex has remained unchanged amidst rapid urban growth and redevelopment, losing its function and becoming an underutilized space in the city center. As a building of historical significance, it must be reimagined not only for preservation but as a civic space that reconnects with the public. By transforming its closed-off layout into an open structure shaped by citizen participation, the space can evolve beyond its original constraints. We propose a new type of recreational complex that reflects diverse lifestyles and the site's unique identity, offering a platform for rest, interaction, learning, and inspiration in everyday urban life.
